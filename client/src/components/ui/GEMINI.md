# GEMINI Playbook — client/src/components/ui

## Purpose
Give Gemini guidance for maintaining UI primitives (buttons, inputs, modals, cards) that underpin the design system.

## How to Work with Requests
1. Identify the primitive to adjust and its shared variants or states.
2. Ensure accessibility (focus states, ARIA labels) and responsiveness are preserved.
3. Keep styling aligned with the Rellio brand and ShadCN/Tailwind conventions.

## Execution Steps
- Define props, variants, and theming options consistent with existing patterns.
- Suggest tests or stories to validate states (hover, active, disabled, loading).
- Note how primitives should support voice/AI-specific affordances (e.g., recording indicators) when relevant.

## Completion Checklist
- Variant and state coverage documented.
- Accessibility and theming requirements captured.
- Reuse guidance provided for downstream components.
