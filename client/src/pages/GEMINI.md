# GEMINI Playbook — client/src/pages

## Purpose
Assist Gemini in shaping route-level compositions that assemble components, hooks, and assets into full Rellio experiences.

## How to Work with Requests
1. Identify the page route and user journey (onboarding, scripture reading, chat, profile, reflections).
2. Determine required data fetching and state management, coordinating with hooks/lib.
3. Ensure pages remain mobile-first, accessible, and performant.

## Execution Steps
- Outline page layout, including the three-pane structure where applicable.
- Specify which components to compose and how to pass data/handlers.
- Note loading/empty/error states and SEO/metadata needs.

## Completion Checklist
- Route purpose and layout defined.
- Data dependencies and states covered.
- Accessibility, performance, and meta requirements documented.
