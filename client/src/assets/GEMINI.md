# GEMINI Playbook — client/src/assets

## Purpose
Support Gemini in organizing in-app assets imported by the React codebase (illustrations, icons, data JSON) that drive UI experiences.

## How to Work with Requests
1. Identify the asset type and its consuming components/pages.
2. Ensure assets are optimized for bundling with Vite (tree-shakable imports, proper formats).
3. Keep naming and folder structure descriptive to aid discoverability.

## Execution Steps
- Recommend placement and naming that reflect persona, scene, or feature usage.
- Suggest optimization or conversion steps (SVG cleanup, responsive image sets) before import.
- Note any TypeScript typings or helper exports needed for consistent consumption.

## Completion Checklist
- Asset purpose and consumer documented.
- Bundling/optimization guidance provided.
- Typings or helper exports added when helpful.
