# client/public/AGENTS.md

## Agent: Client Public (Static Resources)
**Owns:** Static public files (icons, manifests, logos) used across web and mobile deployments.

---

### End Goal Contribution
Provides the **visual identity** of Rellio across devices — ensuring consistency of logos, icons, and favicons across browsers, Android, and iOS.

---

### Tasks Toward End Goal
- Maintain `manifest.json` for PWA installation.
- Add `favicon.ico` and `logo.svg` in different resolutions.
- Generate `apple-touch-icon` variants.
- Define meta tags and theme colors for brand uniformity.
- Auto-sync brand colors with Tailwind configuration.
