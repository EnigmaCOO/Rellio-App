# attached_assets/AGENTS.md

## Agent: Attached Assets (Design & Reference)
**Owns:** Large design files, concept art, exported graphics, or textual assets that are not part of the direct runtime.  
These assets are the *visual and inspirational foundation* of the Rellio experience — informing how the UI, personas, and environments are crafted.

---

### End Goal Contribution
This agent supports the **visual storytelling layer** of Rellio.  
It holds raw design materials, religious art references, and concept iterations that help achieve Rellio’s vision of *immersive spiritual spaces* — each persona’s environment feels sacred, cosmic, and alive.

---

### Tasks Toward End Goal
- Curate symbolic imagery for each faith (mosque interiors, temples, cathedrals, synagogues).
- Generate moodboards for every AI persona’s background (e.g., Islamic Mufti, Hindu Guru).
- Maintain folder structure by theme (`/personas`, `/backgrounds`, `/icons`).
- Convert finalized artwork into optimized assets and move to `/client/public/assets`.
- Add metadata JSONs describing context, color palette, and artist attribution.
- Implement a design ingestion script that maps image paths into the client for automated imports.
