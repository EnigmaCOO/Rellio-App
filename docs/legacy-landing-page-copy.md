## FAQ

**Q: How does the Universal Wisdom Explorer work?**
A: Select a book or persona and Rellio orchestrates scripture, commentary, and AI-guided conversation in a single responsive workspace.

**Q: Can I speak to the personas with my voice?**
A: Yes. Voice prompts stream into the chat, and the persona pauses automatically whenever you speak so the dialogue feels natural and respectful.

**Q: Which traditions are available today?**
A: Christianity, Islam, Judaism, Hinduism, and Buddhism are live now with curated libraries. Additional traditions are on our roadmap.

**Q: Do I need an account to explore?**
A: You can browse highlights without signing in. Creating an account unlocks personalised journeys, saved reflections, and community spaces.
