# GEMINI Playbook — public/images

## Purpose
Guide Gemini in managing production images served by the backend, often for marketing cards, OG previews, or shared visuals.

## How to Work with Requests
1. Identify the image’s use case (OG preview, landing hero, persona illustration).
2. Choose appropriate formats/resolutions and naming aligned with branding.
3. Plan for accessibility: alt text and descriptive captions when referenced.

## Execution Steps
- Recommend optimization (compression, responsive sizes) and verify paths used by consuming pages.
- Suggest folder organization by theme/persona to keep assets discoverable.
- Note any coordination needed with `attached_assets` for source files.

## Completion Checklist
- Image purpose and placement documented.
- Optimized variants recommended.
- Alt text or captions provided where applicable.
