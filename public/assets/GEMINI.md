# GEMINI Playbook — public/assets

## Purpose
Assist Gemini in handling production-ready assets served directly by the backend (images/audio) for marketing or shared contexts.

## How to Work with Requests
1. Identify the asset’s role (marketing hero, download, audio clip) and target consumers.
2. Ensure formats, sizes, and naming align with fast delivery and SEO/preview needs.
3. Keep consistency with branding and avoid duplication with `client/public/assets`.

## Execution Steps
- Recommend optimization steps and any necessary alt text or captions.
- Clarify reference paths used by server-rendered content or external links.
- Suggest organization that keeps persona/scene assets discoverable.

## Completion Checklist
- Optimized asset with clear purpose.
- Reference paths documented.
- Duplication/conflict resolution noted.
